# Airport Cyber Security Report: Illustrative Edition

A 17-page risk framework for a large European airport, using Athens International Airport (ATH) as a named example.

**[Read the report (PDF)](ATH-Cyber-Security-Report.pdf)**

## What it covers

- Public and shuttle-bus Wi-Fi, including evil-twin hotspots
- VPN and remote-access scoping for contractors
- NAT and perimeter hygiene
- Keeping passenger networks completely separate from operational and air traffic systems
- Need-to-know handling of passenger data, EU-domiciled hosting and reduced OS monoculture
- State-linked threats, with documented incidents (NotPetya at Kyiv Boryspil, GPS spoofing over Europe)
- Incident response, a risk matrix and a glossary

## Scope

This is a general, illustrative framework for briefing and training purposes. It is **not** an audit or assessment of Athens International Airport or any other real facility, and no real system was scanned or tested to produce it.
